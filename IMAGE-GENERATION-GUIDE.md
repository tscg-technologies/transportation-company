  `Editorial automotive commercial photography of a sleek unbranded premium executive sedan in dark obsidian finish, parked gracefully at a modern international airport curbside pavilion at dusk. Low 3/4 angle, vehicle situated towards the right side of the frame with subtle cyan and amber rim lighting accentuating aerodynamic shoulder lines.
